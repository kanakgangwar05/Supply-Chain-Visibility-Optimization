# Supply Chain Visibility & Optimization – Milestone 3

## Overview
This milestone focuses on Supplier Performance and Transportation Analytics using Power BI. The dashboards provide insights into supplier reliability, quality performance, transportation efficiency, logistics costs, and shipping performance to support data-driven supply chain decisions.

---

# Supplier Performance Dashboard

## Supplier Scorecard Calculation Methodology

The supplier scorecard evaluates supplier performance using the following KPIs:

- Total Suppliers
- Average Quality Score
- Average Reliability %
- Average Lead Time (Days)
- Products Supplied
- Orders Fulfilled

### Supplier Composite Score

The supplier composite score is calculated using a weighted approach:

- Quality Score – 40%
- Reliability % – 40%
- Lead Time – 20%

This provides an overall performance score for each supplier.

### Reliability Classification

Suppliers are classified into three categories:

- High (Reliability ≥ 80%)
- Medium (Reliability 50–79%)
- Low (Reliability < 50%)

### Supplier Ranking & Benchmarking

Suppliers are ranked using the Supplier Composite Score. Higher-ranked suppliers demonstrate better quality, higher reliability, and shorter lead times, enabling easy comparison across suppliers.

# Transportation Analytics Dashboard

## Transportation Cost Analysis Methodology

The dashboard evaluates transportation efficiency using the following KPIs:

- Average Profit Per Order
- Total Discount Given
- Average Discount Rate
- Same Day Share %
- Late Rate by Shipping Mode

These measures help analyze logistics costs and shipping performance.

### Route & Carrier Performance Evaluation

Transportation performance is analyzed by:

- Shipping Mode
- Market
- Order Region
- Delivery Performance

A heat map highlights regions with higher late delivery rates, while additional visuals compare transportation efficiency across shipping modes.

# Key Insights

- Supplier performance is evaluated using quality, reliability, and lead time metrics.
- Composite scoring helps identify top-performing suppliers.
- Supplier ranking supports benchmarking and vendor selection.
- Transportation analytics identifies the most efficient shipping modes.
- Heat maps highlight regions with higher late delivery rates.
- Discount and profit analysis helps evaluate transportation costs.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Prioritize suppliers with high composite scores.
- Improve supplier relationships with medium-performing vendors.
- Monitor low-performing suppliers for quality and reliability issues.
- Reduce transportation costs by optimizing shipping modes.
- Focus on regions with high late delivery rates to improve logistics performance.
- Continuously monitor KPIs to support better supply chain decision-making.
