Milestone 4 – Warehouse Efficiency & Executive Dashboard
Project Overview

Milestone 4 focuses on developing a Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard and an Executive Dashboard using Power BI. The dashboards provide an overview of warehouse operations, utilization, throughput, inventory efficiency, and overall supply chain performance.

1. Warehouse Utilization Calculation Methodology

Warehouse utilization is calculated using the average utilization percentage of warehouses. Key KPIs include:

Total Warehouses
Average Utilization %
Maximum Utilization %
Minimum Utilization %
Remaining Capacity
Critical Warehouses

A Capacity Risk Flag is used to classify warehouses based on utilization:

Critical: Utilization ≥ 90%
Watch: Utilization ≥ 75%
OK: Utilization < 75%
2. Throughput and Productivity KPI Calculations

Warehouse throughput is evaluated using the number of units shipped through each warehouse.

Key KPIs include:

Warehouse Units Shipped
Units per Order
Total Stock by Warehouse
Total Orders
Remaining Capacity

These measures help evaluate warehouse productivity and identify warehouses that may require better capacity planning.

3. Executive Dashboard Design Methodology

The Executive Dashboard consolidates important KPIs from previous milestones into a single management-level view.

It includes:

Total Orders
Product Categories
Fulfillment Rate
Total Warehouses
Total Suppliers
Dead Stock Quantity
Late Delivery %
Supplier Reliability %
Warehouse Utilization %
Inventory Turnover Ratio
Sales Trend
Regional Sales Analysis

Interactive slicers and drill-through pages allow users to explore the data in more detail.

4. Forecasting Implementation

Sales forecasting was implemented using a Power BI line chart with monthly sales data.

The dashboard includes:

Monthly Sales Trend
Trend Line
Six-Month Forecast
Confidence Interval

This helps identify future sales patterns and supports business planning.

5. Dashboard Optimization Techniques

The dashboards were optimized to improve usability and performance by:

Using reusable DAX measures instead of unnecessary calculated columns.
Applying appropriate data types and aggregations.
Using interactive slicers for filtering.
Implementing drill-through for detailed analysis.
Keeping visuals focused on important KPIs.
Using appropriate charts and KPI cards for clear presentation.
Maintaining consistent formatting and labeling.
6. Key Insights and Business Recommendations

The dashboards help identify:

Warehouses with high utilization and capacity risks.
Differences in warehouse throughput and productivity.
Areas with high dead stock.
Suppliers with different reliability levels.
Regions with higher delivery delays.
Sales trends and expected future performance.

Business Recommendations
Improve capacity planning for warehouses with critical utilization.
Optimize inventory levels to reduce dead stock.
Improve supplier management based on reliability and quality performance.
Monitor regions and shipping modes with high late-delivery rates.
Use sales forecasts to support inventory and operational planning.
Continuously monitor warehouse and supply chain KPIs for better decision-making.
